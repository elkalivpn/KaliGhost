# Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") sets forth the rules and guidelines governing your use of KaliGhost services and platform. By accessing or using our services, you agree to comply with this Policy in addition to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

KaliGhost provides cybersecurity tools and services designed for legitimate security testing, education, and professional use. This Policy establishes acceptable and unacceptable uses of our platform to ensure it is used safely, legally, and ethically.

## 2. Permitted Uses

You may use our services for:

### 2.1 Authorized Security Testing
- Conducting penetration testing on systems you own or have explicit written authorization to test
- Performing vulnerability assessments with proper permissions
- Security research conducted ethically and disclosed responsibly
- Educational purposes in controlled laboratory environments

### 2.2 Learning and Development
- Academic study and training programs
- Skill development in ethical hacking practices
- Practice in controlled lab environments
- Professional certification preparation

### 2.3 Professional Services
- Consulting services for clients with proper contracts
- Internal security assessments for your organization
- Compliance auditing with appropriate authority
- Threat modeling and security architecture design

## 3. Prohibited Activities

You may NOT use our services for any of the following activities:

### 3.1 Unauthorized Access
- Accessing systems, networks, or data without explicit authorization
- Bypassing authentication mechanisms without permission
- Exploiting vulnerabilities in third-party systems without consent
- Circumventing access controls on any system

### 3.2 Illegal Activities
- Violating any applicable laws or regulations
- Engaging in cybercrime or malicious hacking activities
- Distributing illegal content or materials
- Conducting activities that could result in criminal charges

### 3.3 Malicious Actions
- Deploying malware, ransomware, or other harmful code
- Conducting denial-of-service attacks
- Spreading viruses, worms, or other malicious software
- Causing damage to systems or data

### 3.4 Privacy Violations
- Accessing or attempting to access private information without authorization
- Collecting personal data without consent
- Interfering with others' privacy or communications
- Monitoring or intercepting communications unlawfully

### 3.5 Misuse of Resources
- Using our services to launch attacks on third parties
- Consuming excessive resources that impact other users
- Hosting illegal or prohibited content
- Operating botnets or command-and-control infrastructure
- Mining cryptocurrency using our computational resources

### 3.6 Impersonation and Fraud
- Impersonating KaliGhost or its representatives
- Misrepresenting your affiliation with organizations
- Engaging in phishing or social engineering attacks
- Committing fraud or deceptive practices

## 4. Responsible Disclosure

When conducting security research, you agree to:

### 4.1 Ethical Reporting
- Report vulnerabilities responsibly to affected parties
- Follow coordinated disclosure timelines
- Avoid public disclosure before issues are resolved
- Provide sufficient detail for reproduction and remediation

### 4.2 Prohibition on Exploitation
- Not exploit discovered vulnerabilities for personal gain
- Not sell vulnerability information to malicious actors
- Not use vulnerabilities to compromise systems unnecessarily
- Respect reasonable disclosure embargoes

## 5. Educational Use Guidelines

For educational purposes, you must:

### 5.1 Controlled Environments
- Only use our tools in isolated lab environments
- Not target production systems without explicit authorization
- Ensure all testing systems are properly secured and isolated
- Obtain appropriate permissions for all network segments used

### 5.2 Student Conduct
- Adhere to institutional codes of conduct
- Comply with instructor guidelines and course objectives
- Report any accidental findings appropriately
- Understand that academic misconduct policies apply

## 6. Professional Standards

Professional users must maintain industry-standard practices:

### 6.1 Due Diligence
- Verify authorization before conducting any testing
- Maintain detailed scope documentation
- Follow established methodologies and frameworks
- Preserve evidence properly for forensic analysis

### 6.2 Client Relationships
- Maintain clear contracts with defined scope
- Protect client confidentiality appropriately
- Report findings accurately and without embellishment
- Advise clients on remediation priorities

## 7. Monitoring and Enforcement

### 7.1 Activity Monitoring
We may monitor usage patterns to detect:
- Potential violations of this Policy
- Suspicious behavioral indicators
- Resource abuse or misuse
- Attempts to circumvent our controls

### 7.2 Investigation Rights
We reserve the right to:
- Investigate suspected violations of this Policy
- Access and analyze data related to policy violations
- Cooperate with law enforcement when appropriate
- Take corrective action to prevent further violations

### 7.3 Enforcement Actions
Violations may result in:
- Temporary or permanent suspension of access
- Termination of accounts and services
- Legal action and law enforcement referral
- Civil liability for damages caused
