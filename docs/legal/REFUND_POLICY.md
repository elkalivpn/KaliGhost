# Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

This Refund Policy outlines the terms under which you may receive refunds for KaliGhost services, software licenses, and marketplace purchases. Please read this policy carefully before making any purchases.

## 1. Software Licenses

### 1.1 Community Edition
- The Community Edition is free to download and use
- No refunds apply as there is no purchase price

### 1.2 Professional and Enterprise Editions
- **14-Day Money Back Guarantee**: If you're not satisfied with our paid software licenses, you may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase
- Refund requests must be submitted through our support system with justification
- Refunds will not be granted after 14 days except in extraordinary circumstances
- Digital downloads are not eligible for return once accessed

### 1.3 Lifetime Licenses
- Lifetime licenses are non-refundable after 14 days due to their discounted nature
- Within the first 14 days, standard refund policy applies
- Prorated refunds are not available for lifetime licenses

## 2. Subscription Services

### 2.1 KaliGhost Pro Cloud
- Month-to-month subscriptions may be cancelled at any time
- No refunds for partial months of service
- Prepaid annual subscriptions follow the 14-day refund period
- After 14 days, prepaid subscriptions are non-refundable

### 2.2 Enterprise Suite
- Custom enterprise subscriptions follow individual negotiated terms
- Standard 14-day refund period generally applies
- Setup fees and customization charges are typically non-refundable

## 3. Training and Professional Services

### 3.1 Training Programs
- **Pre-Purchase Cancellation**: Full refund available if cancelled 7+ days before scheduled start date
- **Post-Start Cancellation**: No refunds once a training program has commenced
- **No-Show Policy**: No refunds for missed sessions without 24-hour advance notice
- **Rescheduling**: One complimentary reschedule allowed with 48-hour notice

### 3.2 Consulting Services
- Advance deposit payments are fully refundable if service hasn't commenced
- Once work begins, refund eligibility decreases proportionally
- Completed work is non-refundable regardless of results
- Change requests after project commencement may incur additional fees

### 3.3 Support Plans
- Annual support plans may be refunded prorated within 30 days of purchase
- After 30 days, support plans are non-refundable
- Unused support tickets do not extend refund eligibility

## 4. Marketplace Purchases

### 4.1 Digital Extensions
- **14-Day Trial Period**: Most Extensions come with a 14-day satisfaction guarantee
- Refunds require demonstrating functional issues or specification discrepancies
- Refunds not granted for "buyer's remorse" or changed requirements
- Extensions showing signs of use may not qualify for refund

### 4.2 Training Labs and Materials
- Non-consumable digital materials follow standard 14-day policy
- Access to lab environments expires as scheduled and is non-refundable
- Technical issues preventing access may qualify for extension or refund

## 5. Special Circumstances

We may consider refund requests beyond standard terms in cases such as:

### 5.1 Technical Issues
- Critical bugs that prevent core functionality
- Compatibility issues that cannot be resolved
- Performance problems not addressed despite support efforts

### 5.2 Service Unavailability
- Extended platform outages affecting paid services
- Failure to deliver promised features within agreed timeframe
- Unauthorized changes to service specifications

### 5.3 Billing Errors
- Duplicate charges
- Incorrect amounts billed
- Charges for services never ordered or received

## 6. Refund Request Process

To request a refund:

1. Submit request through our support portal at support@kalighost.dev
2. Include your order number and purchase date
3. Explain the reason for your refund request
4. Provide any supporting evidence or documentation
5. Allow 5-10 business days for processing

## 7. Refund Methods

Refunds will be processed using the original payment method whenever possible. If the original payment method is no longer available, alternative arrangements will be made. Processing times vary by payment method:

- Credit cards: 5-10 business days
- Bank transfers: 7-14 business days
- Cryptocurrency: Not available (converted to fiat for refund)
- Gift cards: Balance adjustment only

## 8. Chargebacks

Initiating chargebacks or payment disputes with your financial institution may result in:
- Temporary suspension of your account
- Loss of access to all services
- Potential legal action to recover costs
- Reporting to industry databases

We encourage resolving issues directly through our support system rather than chargebacks.

## 9. Exceptions and Limitations

This refund policy does not apply to:
- Services obtained through unauthorized means
- Purchases from unauthorized resellers
- Services used in violation of our Terms of Service
- Damages resulting from user misconfiguration

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time:
- Material changes will be communicated 30 days in advance
- Existing purchases are governed by the policy in effect at purchase time
- Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of changes
