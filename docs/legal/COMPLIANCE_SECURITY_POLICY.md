# Compliance and Security Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

This Compliance and Security Policy outlines KaliGhost's commitment to maintaining the highest standards of security, privacy, and regulatory compliance in our operations and services.

## 1. Security Framework

### 1.1 Information Security Management
We maintain a comprehensive information security management system based on internationally recognized standards including ISO 27001, NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and CIS Controls.

### 1.2 Risk Management
Our risk management approach includes:
- Annual security risk assessments
- Continuous threat monitoring and analysis
- Incident response planning and testing
- Third-party security assessments
- Regular security awareness training for personnel

### 1.3 Security Controls
Key security controls implemented include:
- Multi-factor authentication for all systems
- End-to-end encryption for data in transit and at rest
- Regular penetration testing and vulnerability assessments
- Automated security monitoring and alerting
- Secure software development lifecycle practices

## 2. Data Protection and Privacy Compliance

### 2.1 GDPR Compliance
For users in the European Union, we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):
- Lawful, fair, and transparent processing of personal data
- Purpose limitation and data minimization principles
- Implementation of appropriate technical and organizational measures
- Data breach notification procedures within 72 hours
- Appointment of Data Protection Officer where required

### 2.2 CCPA Compliance
For California residents, we comply with the California Consumer Privacy Act:
- Clear disclosure of data collection practices
- Consumer rights regarding personal information
- Opt-out mechanisms for data sales (where applicable)
- Non-discrimination for exercise of privacy rights

### 2.3 Global Privacy Requirements
We comply with applicable privacy laws worldwide, including:
- Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD)
- Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)
- Australia's Privacy Act 1988
- Other relevant regional privacy regulations

## 3. Regulatory Compliance

### 3.1 Export Control Compliance
We comply with U.S. export control regulations including:
- Export Administration Regulations (EAR)
- International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) where applicable
- Country embargoes and sanctions programs
- Classification and screening procedures for our technology

### 3.2 Financial Services Compliance
For payment processing and financial operations:
- PCI DSS compliance for cardholder data
- Anti-Money Laundering (AML) program implementation
- Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures where required
- Financial record retention requirements

### 3.3 Industry-Specific Standards
Depending on customer industries, we maintain compliance with:
- HIPAA requirements for healthcare data
- SOX requirements for financial reporting
- FedRAMP standards for government customers
- SOC 2 Type II reporting for service organization controls

## 4. Operational Security Practices

### 4.1 Personnel Security
- Background checks for personnel with elevated access
- Security training for all employees
- Confidentiality agreements for all personnel
- Regular security awareness refreshers

### 4.2 Asset Management
- Inventory and classification of information assets
- Secure disposal of storage media and equipment
- Device and media control procedures
- Return of company assets upon termination

### 4.3 Access Control
- Principle of least privilege implementation
- Regular access reviews and recertification
- Session timeout and automatic lockout mechanisms
- Strong authentication for privileged accounts

### 4.4 Cryptographic Controls
- Use of industry-standard encryption algorithms
- Regular key rotation and management procedures
- Certificate lifecycle management
- Secure key storage and distribution

## 5. Incident Response and Business Continuity

### 5.1 Incident Response
Our incident response program includes:
- 24/7 security monitoring and alerting
- Defined escalation procedures for security incidents
- Coordination with law enforcement and regulatory bodies
- Post-incident analysis and improvement implementation

### 5.2 Business Continuity
To ensure service availability:
- Disaster recovery plan tested quarterly
- Data backup and restoration procedures
- Geographic redundancy for critical systems
- Recovery time objectives documented and maintained

## 6. Third-Party Risk Management

### 6.1 Vendor Assessment
We evaluate third-party vendors based on:
- Security posture and certifications
- Incident response capabilities
- Data protection practices
- Compliance with relevant regulations

### 6.2 Contractual Requirements
Third-party agreements include:
- Security and privacy requirements
- Audit rights and transparency obligations
- Incident notification and response coordination
- Data processing and subcontractor restrictions

### 6.3 Ongoing Monitoring
Continuous vendor oversight through:
- Annual security assessments
- Right to audit and inspection
- Monitoring of security news and disclosures
- Regular review of compliance status

## 7. Audit and Compliance Monitoring

### 7.1 Internal Audits

- Technical security controls effectiveness

- Improvement opportunity identification

### 7.2 External Assessments
Independent verification through:

### 7.3 Continuous Monitoring
Ongoing compliance monitoring includes:

- Security information and event management (SIEM)
- Vulnerability scanning and assessment
- Configuration and change management tracking

### 8.2 Customer Education
