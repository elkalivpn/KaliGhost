## Video Formatting Standards

### Duration 
- Standard tutorials: 7-8 minutes
- Advanced tutorials: 10-12 minutes
- Case studies: 15-20 minutes

### Structure
1. Hook (0:00-0:15): Grab attention
2. Overview (0:15-0:30): What will be covered
3. Step-by-step (0:30-6:00): Main content
4. Summary (6:00-6:30): Key takeaways
5. Call-to-action (6:30-7:00): Next steps

### Caption Standards
- All videos will have English captions
- Captions generated automatically with manual review
- Subtitles available in Spanish and French
